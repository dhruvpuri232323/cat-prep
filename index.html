How to run each block

Spaced revision

  • Each checkbox says which day's learning to revise and which round it is: 1st (next day), 2nd (+3), 3rd (+7), 4th (+14), 5th (+30).
  • Revise cold: close the books, write or say what you remember, then open and mark the gaps. Re-reading doesn't count.
  • Redo only the missed items: Quant Memorise boxes, the grammar exercise lines you got wrong, the WPME words you blanked on.
  • After Dec 1 only revision checkboxes remain, about 5–25 min a day until Dec 27.

Sunday mock

  • One full-length CAT mock from any series you use: VARC, DILR and QA, 40 min each, timed, in one sitting.
  • Then about 45 min of analysis: every wrong or skipped question goes in the Error log tab with its cause, and the scores go in Mock scores.
  • Early scores will be low because DILR and most Quant haven't been covered yet. These mocks build stamina and a baseline.
  • Prefer Saturday? Swap the mock with Saturday's work. Just keep it to one a weekend.

Quant

  • Drill (10 min): the letters are the book's §42 drill menu. Run them in the Quant tab and note the score.
  • Learn: read with a pen, underline the Memorise boxes, then cover-and-recite. Tick the section's Mastery checklist.
  • Log every miss in the Error log tab with its cause (M memory, X method, C calc, R misread, T time) and a one-line fix.

Grammar (Wren & Martin)

  • Pages are the book's printed page numbers.
  • Read the rules, then do at least half of each exercise in writing. Check against the Key if you have it.
  • Log every rule you got wrong. Correct Usage (Comp 17–23) matters most for CAT.
  • Chapters marked skim are low CAT value: read the hints and one specimen only.

Vocab (Word Power)

  • Pages are the PDF page numbers in your file.
  • Do every exercise in the session. Say each word aloud. The book is built for this.
  • Keep a roots list (ego, anthropos, gamos…) and revise it each Sunday.
  • Sessions 18, 38 and 47 are 120-item tests. Note your score.

Exercise

A weekly rotation, best done in the morning before work.

  • Mon / Thu: bodyweight strength
  • Tue / Fri: cardio
  • Wed: yoga or mobility
  • Sat: one longer session
  • Sun: easy walk and stretching

Reading

25 min on weekdays, 45 on weekends: about one book per fortnight. Non-fiction across topics builds CAT reading-comprehension stamina. Type your picks below; they're saved with your ticks.

Minutes are estimates from page counts. If a day overruns, carry the rest into Sunday's catch-up or the final revision days rather than shifting the whole plan.
